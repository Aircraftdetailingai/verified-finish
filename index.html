The Process

Four stages.
Every step verified.

STAGE 01

Wet Sand

Orange peel cannot be polished away — it has to be cut out. Wet sanding levels the surface completely.

STAGE 02

Compound

Compounding removes sanding marks and restores clarity and depth to the paint surface.

STAGE 03

Polish to Verified Gloss

Gloss is measured and documented to confirm it meets the Verified Finish standard before coating.

STAGE 04

Ceramic Coat

Fly Shiny Pro Ceramic Coating applied to a verified surface. Bonds correctly. Performs as intended.
